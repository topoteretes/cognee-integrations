Lenovo’s main competitors and the market segments where they pose the biggest threat

- HP (Hewlett‑Packard)
  - *Segments*: Consumer‑grade laptops & desktops, enterprise workstations & managed‑services contracts, printers.
- Dell Technologies
  - *Segments*: Business‑class PCs, high‑performance workstations, servers + storage, end‑to‑end IT solutions for large enterprises.
- Apple
  - *Segments*: Premium consumer laptops (MacBook) and tablets (iPad) that attract high‑value users away from Windows devices.
- Microsoft (Surface)
  - *Segments*: Premium 2‑in‑1 / convertible devices for enterprise and education, integrated hardware‑software offerings.
- Samsung
  - *Segments*: High‑end tablets and 2‑in‑1 convertibles (Galaxy Book) competing with Lenovo’s Yoga/Tab lines.
- Acer / ASUS
  - *Segments*: Low‑cost consumer notebooks and gaming laptops (Predator/ROG) that pressure Lenovo’s IdeaPad and Legion families.
- Huawei
  - *Segments*: APAC & EMEA smartphones, tablets, and “MateBook”‑style laptops eroding share in emerging regions.
- Regional budget brands (e.g., Xiaomi, local OEMs)
  - *Segments*: Budget and mid‑range notebooks in emerging markets (Asia‑Pacific, LATAM, Africa).
